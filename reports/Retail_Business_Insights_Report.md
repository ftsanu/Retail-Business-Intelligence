# Retail Business Intelligence – Business Insights Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This project analyzes 9,994 retail transactions covering 2014–2017 to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, product categories, discount patterns, and shipping methods.

The business generated approximately **$2.30 million in revenue** and **$286,397 in profit**, with an overall profit margin of **12.47%**.

## 3. Key Business Findings

### 3.1 Regional Performance

- West generated the highest revenue ($725,457.82) and profit ($108,418.45).
- East ranked second in revenue.
- Central recorded the lowest regional profit margin at approximately 7.92%.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the factors behind Central's lower profitability and evaluate whether successful strategies from the West region can be adapted.

### 3.2 Product Category Performance

- Technology generated the highest profit ($145,454.95).
- Office Supplies generated $122,490.81 in profit.
- Furniture generated $741,999.80 in revenue but only $18,451.27 in profit.

**Recommendation:** Review Furniture pricing, product costs, and discount strategies to identify opportunities to improve margins.

### 3.3 Sub-category Profitability

- Copiers generated the highest profit ($55,617.82).
- Tables recorded the largest loss (-$17,725.48).
- Bookcases and Supplies also recorded negative profits.

**Recommendation:** Review pricing, costs, product mix, and promotional strategies for loss-making sub-categories.

### 3.4 Discount Analysis

The analysis found that higher discount levels were associated with weaker profitability in selected sub-categories, particularly Tables and Bookcases.

**Recommendation:** Evaluate discount policies and introduce profitability checks before applying large discounts.

*Note: This analysis identifies associations, not proof of causation.*

### 3.5 Customer Segment Performance

- Consumer generated the highest revenue ($1,161,401.35).
- Consumer also generated the highest total profit ($134,119.21).
- Home Office had the highest profit margin at approximately 14.03%.

**Recommendation:** Maintain strategies that support Consumer revenue while exploring opportunities to grow the more profitable Home Office segment.

### 3.6 Monthly Sales Trends

- November 2017 recorded the highest monthly revenue ($118,447.83).
- December 2016 recorded the highest monthly profit ($17,885.31).

**Recommendation:** Use historical monthly performance to inform seasonal sales planning and inventory management.

### 3.7 Shipping Performance

- Standard Class accounted for the highest order volume (2,994 orders).
- First Class recorded the highest profit margin (13.93%).
- Standard Class had an average order-to-ship interval of 5.01 days.

**Recommendation:** Evaluate shipping costs and service levels together when selecting shipping strategies.

*Note: Shipping Days represents the interval between Order Date and Ship Date, not customer delivery time.*

## 4. Overall Business Recommendations

1. Investigate low-profit regions and product categories.
2. Review discount policies for loss-making sub-categories.
3. Explore opportunities to grow the Home Office customer segment.
4. Use historical monthly trends to support sales planning.
5. Evaluate shipping methods based on both cost and profitability.
6. Monitor revenue and profit together rather than relying on sales volume alone.

## 5. Conclusion

The project demonstrates how Python, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI can be combined to transform retail transaction data into actionable business insights.

The findings highlight opportunities to improve profitability, optimize discount strategies, understand customer segments, and support data-driven decision-making.
